# Code of conduct

Couchtop is a friendly, hobby-sized project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented) is expected to help keep it that way.

## Be kind

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to programming.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing conversations
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Sexualized content or unwelcome advances
- Posting copyrighted or trademarked console assets, cracks, or anything illegal

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
[security advisory form](https://github.com/TheAgencyMGE/couchtop/security/advisories/new)
(it's private) or by messaging [@TheAgencyMGE](https://github.com/TheAgencyMGE).
Reports are handled confidentially.
